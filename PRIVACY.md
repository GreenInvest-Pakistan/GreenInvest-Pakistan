# GreenInvest Pakistan privacy summary

## Information used for a calculation

GreenInvest processes the decision inputs entered in the calculator, such as
location, consumption, bill target, budget, roof preference, backup requirement,
and proposed equipment. These values are needed to produce the requested result.

The official Windows application performs calculations locally. The hosted
calculator sends a submitted configuration to the GreenInvest calculation
service and keeps only a small, time-limited in-memory result cache for reliable
repeat requests. Drafts and the latest completed result remain in storage on the
consumer's device.

## Anonymous project records

After a completed assessment, the official service can record a minimal
anonymous project event for aggregate impact reporting. It can include:

- random salted analysis and session identifiers;
- selected city and broad residential or commercial segment;
- recommendation or quote-check journey and outcome group;
- selected solar, inverter, and battery capacities;
- approximate screened system value; and
- counts of configurations and sizes evaluated.

These records do not contain a name, email address, IP address, user agent,
account number, raw bill, monthly demand profile, budget, roof details,
appliance list, private quotation document, or downloaded plan.

Public impact pages return aggregates only. They do not return individual
events or optional comments. Project figures describe recorded assessments;
they do not prove purchases, installations, realised savings, or causal impact.

## Optional feedback

Feedback is optional. Survey choices and an optional comment are sent only after
the consumer selects the evaluation permission option and submits. Do not enter
names, addresses, account numbers, bill details, or other identifying material
in a comment.

## Third parties

The official interface does not use an advertising SDK, tracking pixel,
consumer account, or third-party analytics cookie. The hosted service and public
release downloads necessarily use their hosting providers' normal network and
security infrastructure.

Questions or deletion requests concerning a submitted comment can be raised
through the [support process](SUPPORT.md) without posting the comment publicly.
