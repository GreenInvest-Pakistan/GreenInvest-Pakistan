# Consumer tariff reference

GreenInvest Pakistan 4.5.0 uses the **GreenInvest 2026 LESCO reference
schedule** for residential, commercial, and industrial calculations. The
calculator selects the applicable row from the consumer's connection type,
monthly use, connected load, and connection phase.

## Residential

| Monthly consumption | Energy rate |
| --- | ---: |
| 0–50 units | PKR 3.95/kWh |
| 51–100 units | PKR 7.74/kWh |
| 101–200 units | PKR 9.82/kWh |
| 201–300 units | PKR 14.37/kWh |
| 301–400 units | PKR 16.29/kWh |
| 401–500 units | PKR 20.88/kWh |
| 501–600 units | PKR 25.43/kWh |
| 601–700 units | PKR 38.41/kWh |
| Above 700 units | PKR 47.69/kWh |

The reference fixed charge is PKR 75 per month for a single-phase connection
and PKR 150 per month for a three-phase connection.

## What may still appear on the bill

The reference schedule is the calculation starting point, not a reproduction
of every payable line. A real bill may also contain taxes, fuel-cost and
quarterly adjustments, duties, surcharges, television fees, meter or service
charges, arrears, minimum-bill rules, and other utility-specific items. Compare
the selected category, rates, and phase with a recent electricity bill before
making a purchase decision.

GreenInvest is independent decision support. It is not LESCO, NEPRA, an
electricity supplier, or a substitute for confirmation by the relevant utility.
