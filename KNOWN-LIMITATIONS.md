# Known limitations

- GreenInvest is a screening model, not a site survey, structural assessment,
  electrical design, installer quotation, warranty, financing offer, or tax
  calculation.
- The displayed Pakistan consumption-slab rates and fixed grid charges do not
  represent every possible bill line. Taxes, fuel adjustments, quarterly
  adjustments, duties, surcharges, television fees, arrears, minimum charges,
  and utility-specific items can raise the payable bill.
- Tariffs, net-metering or export rules, equipment availability, market prices,
  and government policy can change after a release.
- Solar resource and seasonal demand are estimates. Shade, orientation, dust,
  temperature, equipment mismatch, downtime, weather, and behaviour can change
  actual production and consumption.
- A battery's useful backup depends on the real protected load, starting surge,
  power factor, depth of discharge, temperature, degradation, inverter limits,
  installation quality, and outage duration.
- A desired bill, budget, roof limit, or backup requirement can be mutually
  incompatible. A conditional or do-not-install result is a valid outcome.
- Sensitivity and Monte Carlo outputs describe the model's stated screening
  ranges. They are not forecasts or guaranteed probabilities.
- The Windows preview is not code-signed and may trigger SmartScreen. It supports
  64-bit Windows 10 and 11; phones should use the hosted calculator.
- Device-local drafts and results are not cross-device accounts or cloud backups.
- Public project metrics represent centrally recorded assessments, not audited
  national adoption, completed purchases, or installed capacity.

Always obtain itemised proposals and confirm the final design, protection,
earthing, roof structure, warranties, utility treatment, and complete payable
bill before purchase.
